# MekaCycle: The Mad Scientist Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for when you're in the middle of a pile of "junk."

## 🔌 Power Rules

- **Red/Black wires**: Usually DC power (Voltage varies).
- **USB**: Always 5V (Red = +, Black = -).
- **LiPo Batteries**: Nominal 3.7V. Full at 4.2V. Dead at 3.0V. **NEVER** puncture.
- **Laptop PSUs**: Usually 19V. Great for DC-DC buck converters to get any voltage you need.

## 🛠️ Essential "Scavenger" Tools

1. **Multimeter**: Your most important tool. Measure voltage and continuity.
2. **Phillips #0-2**: Most electronics use these.
3. **Torx T5-T10**: Essential for modern phones and tablets.
4. **Soldering Iron**: To rescue components from those green PCBs.
5. **Flush Cutters**: To snip components off quickly.

## 🧪 Quick Component Testing

- **LEDs**: Use a 3V coin cell battery (CR2032). Long leg = Positive.
- **Capacitors**: Look for "Bulging" tops. If they aren't flat, they are trash.
- **Switches**: Use the "Beep" (Continuity) mode on your multimeter to find if a button still works.
- **Magnets**: Find them inside Hard Drives and Speakers. Incredibly strong.

## ☠️ The "No-Go" Zone

- **Microwave Capacitors**: Can KILL you even if unplugged for weeks.
- **CRT Anodes**: High voltage storage. Requires grounding probe.
- **Mercury Switches**: Found in very old thermostats. Toxic if broken.
