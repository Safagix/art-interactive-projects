# MekaCycle Salvage Catalog v1.0

This catalog lists common consumer electronics and the "gold" they contain for robotics and mecatronics.

## 🖨️ Printers (The Motherlode)

Printers are the best source of precision components.

- **Laser Printers**: High-torque stepper motors, linear shafts, beefy 24V power supplies, high-power cooling fans.
- **Inkjet Printers**: Smaller DC motors (often geared), timing belts, optical encoder strips/disks, small solenoids.
- **Scanners**: Linear shafts, long timing belts, stepper motors, glass plates (great for 3D printer beds).

## 📼 VCRs & DVD Players

Though becoming rarer, they are great for mechanical parts.

- **VCR Cabinet**: High-quality metal sheets for chassis.
- **Internal Mechanisms**: Tiny pulleys, belts, springs, and gears.
- **VCR Drum**: High-quality bearing assembly (can be used for rotating platforms).
- **DVD/CD Drives**: High-precision slim stepper motors (perfect for mini CNCs/Laser engravers).

## 📱 Smartphones & Tablets

High tech, but difficult to harvest.

- **Vibration Motors**: Tiny "pancake" or "coin" motors for haptic feedback.
- **Li-Po Batteries**: 3.7V power sources (check for swelling/damage!).
- **Speakers/Mics**: Very high quality for their size.
- **Backlit Panels**: Can be salvaged for light boxes or subtle UI effects.

## 🖥️ PC Towers & Peripherals

- **Power Supplies (ATX)**: The ultimate bench power supply (3.3V, 5V, 12V rails).
- **Hard Drives**: Very strong Neodymium magnets and high-speed brushless motors.
- **Mice**: IR sensors and tiny microswitches.
- **Keyboards**: Membrane switches and sometimes high-quality mechanical switches.

## 📺 Monitors & TVs

- **LCD/LED**: The logic board might have usable voltage regulators. The stand is often a solid piece of plastic/metal.
- **⚠️ CRT Monitors**: DO NOT OPEN unless you are an expert. Discharge the anode first. Contain heavy lead and high-voltage capacitors.
