# The Frankenstein Laboratory: Initial Project Ideas

These are examples of "radical upcycling" projects that MekaCycle can help you build using salvaged parts.

## 1. The "Scanner-Plotter"

- **Ingredients**: 1x Old Flatbed Scanner, 1x DVD Drive, 1x Arduino/ESP32, 2x Stepper Drivers.
- **Concept**: Use the scanner's long linear axis as the X-axis and the DVD drive's laser-head carrier as the Y-axis.
- **Application**: A 2D drawing robot or a low-power laser engraver.

## 2. The "Printer-Bot" Chassis

- **Ingredients**: 1x Large Inkjet Printer Frame, 2x Gearbox Motors (from toys), 1x L298N Motor Driver.
- **Concept**: Most printers have a very sturdy plastic or aluminum base. Strip the mechanicals and flip it over to use as a heavy-duty robot chassis.
- **Application**: A robust mobile platform for exploration or delivery.

## 3. The "Haptic Controller"

- **Ingredients**: 10x Vibration motors (from junk phones), 1x Glove, 1x Microcontroller.
- **Concept**: Sew the tiny coin motors onto the fingertips of a glove.
- **Application**: VR feedback or a "navigation glove" for the visually impaired.

## 4. The "Bench Power Lab"

- **Ingredients**: 1x ATX PC Power Supply, 1x Project Box, 1x Voltmeter Module.
- **Concept**: Convert a discarded PC power supply into a lab-grade unit with stable 3.3V, 5V, and 12V outputs.
- **Application**: Powering all your future robotics projects.

## 5. The "Phone-Vision" Turret

- **Ingredients**: 1x Old Smartphone (with working camera), 2x Servos, 1x 3D printed/wooden bracket.
- **Concept**: Use the old phone as the "brain" and "eyes." Connect it via Bluetooth/Serial to a microcontroller that moves the servos.
- **Application**: An AI-powered security turret or a face-tracking camera rig.
