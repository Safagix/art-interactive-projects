Air Lab — Gesture Guide
☝️
Draw Schema
Point index finger to draw. Keep other fingers curled.
🖐️
Compile Hologram
Hold palm open & still for 0.5s to convert drawing into a 3D hologram.
🤏
Grab / Assemble
Pinch near an object to grab. Drag near another & release to snap together.
💨
Disassemble
Swipe quickly across a group to break it apart. Swipe a solo object to delete.
🤲
Two-Hand Zoom
Show both hands. Spread apart to zoom in, bring together to zoom out.
🧊
Shape Buttons
Use the left toolbar to spawn Cube, Sphere, Cylinder, or Torus primitives.
